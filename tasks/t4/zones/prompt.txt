Create a kernel with stable framerate that draws three moving objects on a black background: a white ($0E) ship, a yellow ($1E) rock and a green ($C4) UFO. Positions are those of an image's top-left corner: the left end of its top row is at pixel x (0-159, counting from the left edge) on visible line y (0-191, counting from the top). Frames are counted from 0, the first frame drawn.

On frame f, the ship is at x = f mod 153, y = 16, and the UFO at x = (100 + 2f) mod 153. The rock is always at x = 76. The rock's y is 56 on frame 0, and goes down 1 line a frame to 104, then back up 1 line a frame to 56, and so on. The UFO's y is 176 on frame 0, and goes up 1 line a frame to 144, then back down 1 line a frame to 176, and so on.
